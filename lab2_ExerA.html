About the Event

The Faculty Programming Workshop 2026 is a one-day event designed to help students improve their programming skills, explore new technologies, and learn from experienced speakers.

Speaker 1: Dr. Ahmad Rahman

Dr. Ahmad Rahman is a software engineer and lecturer who specialises in web development. He will introduce students to the fundamentals of HTML and web programming.

Speaker 2: Ms. Nur Aisyah

Ms. Nur Aisyah is an experienced application developer. She will conduct a hands-on programming session and share useful tips for developing software projects.
